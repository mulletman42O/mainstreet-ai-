# MainStreet AI

Free, open-source AI automation for small businesses, built to run on the phone you already have.

## Why this exists

Big companies have entire teams building AI tools. Small business owners have a phone and not enough hours in the day. MainStreet AI closes that gap.

Everything here is free, open source, and written in plain English so anyone can set it up. No coding background required.

## What it does

The goal: handle the busywork so you can make more money, do less grunt work, and spend more time with your family.

## Roadmap

- [ ] **Missed call text back:** instantly texts callers you couldn't answer with a booking link
- [ ] **Review requests:** sends a review request automatically after a job is done
- [ ] **Job photos to social posts:** turns job photos into ready-to-post captions
- [ ] **Quote follow-ups:** drafts a friendly follow-up when a quote goes unanswered
- [ ] **Overnight phone agent:** runs tasks on a plugged-in phone while you sleep
- [ ] **Public website** with setup guides and video walkthroughs

## Who's building it

Built by Tate, owner of a water filtration company and a holiday lights company in Iowa. Every workflow gets tested on real businesses before it gets published here.
